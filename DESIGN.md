## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a modern, high-precision e-commerce experience tailored for technology and lifestyle verticals in South Asia. Taking structural inspiration from Apple Store’s tactile minimalism, Shopify’s commerce clarity, and Linear’s micro-detailed craft, the visual ethos balances warmth with authoritative engineered restraint.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Airy Precision:** Generous negative space punctuated by sharp typographic cadence and soft boundary lines.
- **Warm Substrate:** Stone-tinted neutrals eliminate sterile coldness, providing an organic foundation for luminous deep-teal accents.
- **Tactile Modernism:** Subtle surface shifts, 1px perimeter definition, and gentle elevation layers create responsive depth without visual noise.
- **Regional Dignity:** Indian Rupee (`₹`) styling with standard Indian digit grouping (e.g., `₹1,45,000`) integrated seamlessly into typographic hierarchies.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around calibrated contrast between deep oceanic teal and warm stone mineral foundations.

### Brand Role Spectrum
- **Primary Light (`#0F766E`):** A deep, reassuring teal chosen for purchase CTAs, prominent active filters, brand icons, and high-emphasis links. On-primary text is pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Primary Dark (`#2DD4BF`):** A high-vibrancy mint-teal tuned for dark backgrounds to ensure WCAG AAA accessibility. Text on dark primary uses ultra-deep teal (`#042F2E`).
- **Accent Amber (`#F59E0B` light / `#FBBF24` dark):** Reserved strictly for functional friction: star rating scores and "Low Stock" indicators. Never used for marketing fluff.
- **Semantic Suite:** Standardized across transactional life-cycles (Pending neutral `#78716C`, Confirmed/Info `#0369A1`/`#38BDF8`, Shipped/Primary Teal, Delivered/Success `#15803D`/`#4ADE80`, Cancelled/Danger `#DC2626`/`#F87171`).

### Surface Strategy
Dark mode relies on stepped surface luminance (`#0C0A09` canvas to `#1C1917` card containers to `#292524` nested fills) bounded by crisp `#34302D` borders, avoiding murky drop shadows.

## Typography

The type scale uses Inter across all viewports to project technological cleanliness and geometric consistency.

- **Numerals & Currencies:** The Indian Rupee glyph (`₹`) uses the exact weight and vertical metrics of the adjoining figures. Tabular figures (`tnum`) must be enforced for price displays, checkout breakdowns, stock steppers, and admin data tables to prevent layout jitter.
- **Tracking Discipline:** Tighten tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) on display sizes to retain density on wide screens.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Category labels on cards use `caption` styling with medium weight, while headings prioritize legibility and truncate cleanly after two lines maximum.

## Layout & Spacing

A responsive 12-column fluid grid system pinned to a maximum container width of `1440px`.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1024px – 1440px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, `2rem` (32px) page margin. Product grids conform to 4 columns.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `1.5rem` outer margins. Product grids collapse to 3 columns.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1rem` margins. Product grids collapse to 2 columns with reduced component padding.

### Structural Rhythms
- **Header:** Sticky, fixed `64px` height with backdrop blur (`blur(12px)`).
- **Vertical Flow:** Major layout zones use `space-xl` separation, while related form controls and data rows adhere to `space-sm` and `space-md` cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through layered tonal planes and diffused, low-contrast shadows rather than heavy structural drop-shadows.

### Layering Hierarchy
1. **Base Layer (L0):** Canvas substrate (`#FAFAF9` light / `#0C0A09` dark).
2. **Container Layer (L1):** Cards, sticky elements, and sidebars (`#FFFFFF` light / `#1C1917` dark) framed with a 1px border (`#E7E5E4` light / `#34302D` dark).
3. **Elevated Surfaces (L2):** Flyouts, dropdown menus, context popovers, and sticky order summaries. In light mode: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.05)`. In dark mode: 1px border illumination (`#3E3A37`) with zero ambient shadow.
4. **Modal & Drawer Overlays (L3):** High-priority alerts and slide-over drawers with backdrop scrim (`rgba(12, 10, 9, 0.4)` light / `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)` dark with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`).

### Glassmorphism Treatment
The global navigation bar uses semi-transparent backing (`rgba(250, 250, 249, 0.85)` light / `rgba(12, 10, 9, 0.85)` dark) coupled with a `12px` Gaussian blur and a hairline bottom edge border.

## Shapes

The geometric signature uses deliberate corner radius steps to communicate function and physical hierarchy:

- **12px (`rounded-lg`):** Reserved for product cards, cart order summaries, promotional banner blocks, modal panels, and checkout segment cards.
- **8px (`rounded`):** Strict boundary for interactive elements: buttons, input fields, dropdown triggers, and numeric steppers.
- **Pill (Fully Rounded / 9999px):** Status badges (Pending, Delivered, Out of Stock), category chips, cart item counters, and theme toggles.
- **Avatar & Stepper Dots:** Circular geometry (`rounded-full`) for user profiles and checkout tracker indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0F766E` (Light) / `#2DD4BF` (Dark). White text (Light) / `#042F2E` (Dark). Height 40px, corner radius 8px, padding `0 1rem`. Hover transitions to `#115E59` / `#5EEAD4`.
- **Secondary:** Surface fill `#F5F5F4` (Light) / `#292524` (Dark), text color neutral-900.
- **Ghost:** Borderless, background transparent, active hover reveals stone subtle fill.
- **Danger:** Background `#DC2626` (Light) / `#F87171` (Dark) with white text, strictly for irreversible operations like order cancellation.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 42px, radius 8px, border 1px `#E7E5E4` (Light) / `#34302D` (Dark). Focus state applies a 1px border `#0F766E` with a 2px outer ring in teal tint (`#F0FDFA`).
- **Inline Validation:** Red error text (`#DC2626` / `#F87171`) sits 4px below the field. Error fields tint their border to Danger.
- **Quantity Stepper:** Unified 8px container encompassing decrement, tabular numeric value, and increment buttons.

### Product Cards
- **Image Area:** 4:3 fixed aspect ratio with subtle zoom on hover. Top right overlay hosts the wishlist heart icon (circular 32px hit area).
- **Meta Block:** Category caption in muted tone, title restricted to 2 lines, price highlighted in bold tabular figures.
- **List-View Constraint:** Star ratings are omitted in product list cards to maintain scan speed. Add-to-cart buttons appear via bottom slide or hover reveal on desktop.
- **Out of Stock:** Reduced card opacity (50%), with an absolute centered pill badge.

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Status Pills:** Height 24px, pill-shaped, uppercase caption type. Background is a 10% tint of the semantic color with matching solid foreground text.
- **Category Chips:** Light stone fill, hover transitions to subtle teal tint, active state applies solid teal background with white text.

### Checkout & Order Cards
- **Selectable Option Cards:** Address and Payment tiles feature 12px corners, radio controls, and an explicit 1.5px primary teal border when selected.
